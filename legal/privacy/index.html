Who We Are

DateVault operates a private, invite-only online dating membership platform. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and your rights regarding your information.

By applying for, paying for, or using a DateVault membership, you consent to the collection and processing of your personal data as described in this Policy.

◈  We take privacy seriously. DateVault is a curated private community — your data is not sold to advertisers and your profile is only visible to other verified members.

03

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to operate and improve the DateVault platform. Specifically:

  • To verify your identity and process your membership application.
  • To display your profile to other verified members for matching purposes.
  • To facilitate matching and messaging between members.
  • To process and record your payment and activate your membership.
  • To send you transactional emails, including application status notifications, invite codes, and payment confirmations.
  • To send admin notifications via an internal messaging tool (internal operations only — not visible to members).
  • To review reports of abusive or harmful behaviour and enforce our Terms.
  • To provide customer support via in-app support tickets.
  • To maintain platform security and prevent fraud, fake profiles, and abuse.
  • To manage your subscription, including billing cycle tracking, plan enforcement, feature access gating, and sending renewal or expiry notifications.
  • To process Priority Support requests and schedule video calls with the admin team upon payment confirmation.

We do not use your data for advertising, behavioural profiling for commercial purposes, or sale to third parties.

04

Identity Verification Data

During the application process, we require a government-issued photo ID and a selfie holding that ID. This is critical to our mission of maintaining a verified, authentic member community.

◈  Your verification documents are never shared with other members. They are reviewed only by the DateVault admin team for the purpose of identity verification.

Verification documents are:

  • Stored securely via our authorised media storage provider with restricted access.
  • Accessible only to authorised DateVault admin accounts.
  • Used solely to confirm that you are who you say you are.
  • Retained for as long as your account is active, and deleted upon account deletion.
05

Photos & Media

Your profile photos are stored with a third-party cloud media storage provider and displayed to other verified members as part of your profile.

You may add, remove, or change your profile photos at any time via your profile settings. Removed photos are deleted from your profile, though the storage provider's own retention policies may apply to cached copies.

Please do not upload photos of other people without their consent, or photos that contain personally identifying information of third parties.

06

Payment Data

DateVault does not store your card numbers, bank account details, or mobile money credentials. All payment processing is handled by authorised third-party payment processors. The payment method presented to you will depend on your location — members in different regions are shown relevant local payment options.

We receive and store only the outcome of your payment (success/failure), the transaction reference number, and the payment method type. This is used to activate your membership and maintain our payment records.

For details on how your payment data is handled during processing, please refer to the privacy policy of the payment processor applicable to your transaction.

07

Communications & Messages

Private messages between matched members are stored securely in our database. Only the two participants in a match can read their conversation. Admin accounts have read access to messages for the purpose of:

  • Investigating reports of harassment or abusive behaviour.
  • Resolving support tickets where message context is relevant.
  • Enforcing compliance with our Terms & Conditions.

DateVault does not actively monitor or read member messages unless a report or complaint is under investigation.

Transactional emails are sent via our authorised email delivery provider. Your email address is shared with this provider solely for the purpose of delivering these emails. Admin notifications are sent via an internal messaging tool used exclusively by our admin team and do not expose your information publicly.

09

Data Sharing

Your personal information is shared as follows:

  • With other members: Your profile information (name, age, photos, bio, interests, zodiac, relationship goals, location) is visible to other verified active members. Your email, verification documents, and payment details are never shared with other members.
  • With our admin team: Full profile data, verification documents, messages (when investigating reports), and payment records are accessible to authorised DateVault administrators.
  • With third-party service providers: As described in Section 8, limited data is shared with providers solely to deliver their services.
  • With law enforcement: We may disclose data if required by law, court order, or government authority, or where we believe disclosure is necessary to protect the safety of DateVault, our members, or the public.

We do not share, rent, sell, or trade your personal data with any third party for their independent marketing or commercial purposes.

10

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide our services.

  • Active accounts: All data retained while your membership is active.
  • Voluntarily deleted accounts: Profile data, photos, and verification documents deleted within 30 days of a confirmed deletion request.
  • Banned accounts: A minimal record (email, ban reason, ban date) is retained indefinitely to prevent re-registration in violation of our Terms.
  • Rejected applications: Application data may be retained for up to 90 days for fraud prevention, then deleted.
  • Payment records: Transaction records retained for up to 7 years for legal and accounting compliance.
  • Subscription records: Plan history, billing dates, and status changes are retained for up to 7 years for accounting and dispute resolution purposes.
  • Priority Support requests: Retained for up to 2 years for internal records and dispute resolution.

Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Update inaccurate profile information directly in your profile settings, or contact support for assistance.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and personal data (see Section 10 for retention exceptions).
  • Objection: Object to certain processing of your data if you believe it is being used unlawfully.
  • Portability: Request a structured export of your personal data in a machine-readable format.
ℹ  To exercise any of these rights, contact us via the in-app support feature. We will respond within 30 days. Some requests may be subject to legal retention obligations.
12

Security

We implement reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data. These measures include:

  • Authentication provider — industry-standard authentication with encrypted password storage.
  • Database security rules — strictly enforced rules ensure members can only access data they are permitted to see. Other members cannot access your verification documents, payment records, or messages outside your shared matches.
  • Secure media storage — photos and documents stored with access restricted to authorised personnel.
  • Server-side secrets — API keys for payment providers, email, and notification services are stored only on our secure backend and never exposed in client-side code.
  • HTTPS — all data is transmitted over encrypted connections.

While we take security seriously, no system is completely secure. You are responsible for keeping your account password confidential. Report any suspected unauthorised access immediately via our support feature.
